### EQ-SAMP-LHS-001 — Latin hypercube sampling

`X_ij = (pi_j(i) + V_ij)/n`, where `pi_j` are independent random permutations
of `{0,...,n-1}` and `V_ij ~ Uniform(0,1)` (or `1/2` when centered).

- Domain: positive `n` and `d`.
- Meaning: every one-dimensional projection has exactly one point per
  stratum; the mean estimator is unbiased with variance never much worse than
  Monte Carlo and much smaller for additive integrands.
- Code: `sampling.latin_hypercube`.
- Source: [REF-MCKAY-BECKMAN-CONOVER-1979] and
  [REF-OWEN-2013, Section 10.3].
  - [REF-MCKAY-BECKMAN-CONOVER-1979](../references.md#ref-mckay-beckman-conover-1979): M. D. McKay, R. J. Beckman, and W. J. Conover. "A Comparison of Three Methods for Selecting Values of Input Variables in the Analysis of Output from a Computer Code." *Technometrics* 21(2), 239-245, 1979.
  - [REF-OWEN-2013](../references.md#ref-owen-2013): Art B. Owen. *Monte Carlo Theory, Methods and Examples*. 2013. [https://artowen.su.domains/mc/](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/).
- Registry: [EQ-SAMP-LHS-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-samp-lhs-001--latin-hypercube-sampling)

### EQ-SAMP-HALTON-001 — Radical inverse and Halton sequence

`phi_b(i) = sum_k d_k(i) b^-(k+1)` for base-`b` digits `d_k(i)`; the Halton
point is `(phi_(p_1)(i), ..., phi_(p_d)(i))` for the first `d` primes. A
scrambled version replaces `d_k` by `pi(d_k)` with `pi(0) = 0`.

- Domain: non-negative integer index, dimension `<= 50`.
- Meaning: deterministic low-discrepancy points; quality degrades in high
  dimension, which scrambling partly mitigates.
- Code: `sampling.radical_inverse`, `sampling.halton`.
- Source: [REF-HALTON-1960] and [REF-OWEN-2013, Section 15.4].
  - [REF-HALTON-1960](../references.md#ref-halton-1960): J. H. Halton. "On the Efficiency of Certain Quasi-Random Sequences of Points in Evaluating Multi-Dimensional Integrals." *Numerische Mathematik* 2, 84-90, 1960.
  - [REF-OWEN-2013](../references.md#ref-owen-2013): Art B. Owen. *Monte Carlo Theory, Methods and Examples*. 2013. [https://artowen.su.domains/mc/](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/).
- Registry: [EQ-SAMP-HALTON-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-samp-halton-001--radical-inverse-and-halton-sequence)

### EQ-SAMP-SOBOL-001 — Sobol digital sequence (Gray-code construction)

`m_k = 2a_1 m_(k-1) xor 4a_2 m_(k-2) xor ... xor 2^s m_(k-s) xor m_(k-s)`,
`v_k = m_k / 2^k`, and `x_(i+1) = x_i xor v_(c(i))`, where `c(i)` is the index
of the lowest zero bit of `i` and `a_j` are coefficients of a primitive
polynomial of degree `s`.

- Domain: dimension `<= 16` with the bundled primitive polynomials and
  initial direction numbers; `2^m` points give the full net property.
- Meaning: a `(t,m,d)`-net whose one-dimensional projections are perfectly
  stratified at every power of two.
- Code: `sampling.sobol`.
- Source: [REF-SOBOL-1967], [REF-BRATLEY-FOX-1988], and [REF-JOE-KUO-2008].
  - [REF-SOBOL-1967](../references.md#ref-sobol-1967): I. M. Sobol'. "On the Distribution of Points in a Cube and the Approximate Evaluation of Integrals." *USSR Computational Mathematics and Mathematical Physics* 7(4), 86-112, 1967.
  - [REF-BRATLEY-FOX-1988](../references.md#ref-bratley-fox-1988): Paul Bratley and Bennett L. Fox. "Algorithm 659: Implementing Sobol's Quasirandom Sequence Generator." *ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software* 14(1), 88-100, 1988.
  - [REF-JOE-KUO-2008](../references.md#ref-joe-kuo-2008): Stephen Joe and Frances Y. Kuo. "Constructing Sobol Sequences with Better Two-Dimensional Projections." *SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing* 30(5), 2635-2654, 2008.

### EQ-SAMP-RQMC-001 — Randomized quasi-Monte Carlo

Cranley-Patterson shift `x' = (x + Delta) mod 1` with `Delta ~ U(0,1)^d`, or
digital shift `x' = x xor Delta` for base-2 nets.

- Domain: any deterministic point set on `[0,1)^d`.
- Meaning: each randomized point is uniform, so the estimator is unbiased;
  replicates give an honest error estimate while low discrepancy is kept.
- Code: `sampling.halton(shift=True)`, `sampling.sobol(scramble=True)`,
  `propagation.compare_designs`.
- Source: [REF-CRANLEY-PATTERSON-1976] and [REF-L-ECUYER-2018].
  - [REF-CRANLEY-PATTERSON-1976](../references.md#ref-cranley-patterson-1976): R. Cranley and T. N. L. Patterson. "Randomization of Number Theoretic Methods for Multiple Integration." *SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis* 13(6), 904-914, 1976.
  - [REF-L-ECUYER-2018](../references.md#ref-l-ecuyer-2018): Pierre L'Ecuyer. "Randomized Quasi-Monte Carlo: An Introduction for Practitioners." In *Monte Carlo and Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods (MCQMC 2016)*, Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics 241, 29-52, 2018.
- Also names: [`propagation`](propagation.md)
- Registry: [EQ-SAMP-RQMC-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-samp-rqmc-001--randomized-quasi-monte-carlo)

### EQ-SAMP-DISC-001 — Grid lower bound on star discrepancy

`D*_grid = max_(t in G) | #{x_i in [0,t)}/n - prod_j t_j |` over anchored boxes
whose corners lie on a regular grid `G`.

- Domain: dimension `<= 4` for tractable enumeration.
- Meaning: a lower bound on the star discrepancy that ranks designs of equal
  size; it is not the exact discrepancy.
- Code: `sampling.star_discrepancy_bound`.
- Source: [REF-OWEN-2013, Section 15.2].
  - [REF-OWEN-2013](../references.md#ref-owen-2013): Art B. Owen. *Monte Carlo Theory, Methods and Examples*. 2013. [https://artowen.su.domains/mc/](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/).

### EQ-SAMP-HALTON-002 — Randomized Halton by digit permutations

`x = sum_(k=1..K) pi_(j,k)(d_k(i)) b_j^-k + U b_j^-K`, with independent uniform
permutations `pi_(j,k)` of `{0, ..., b_j - 1}` for every axis `j` and digit
position `k` (zero included), `b_j^-K <= 2^-53`, `U ~ U(0,1)`.

- Domain: prime bases; leading zero digits are permuted too.
- Meaning: every point is uniform on the unit cube (unbiased estimators)
  while van der Corput strata are preserved.
- Code: `sampling.halton`.
- Source: [REF-MATOUSEK-1998], [REF-OWEN-1995].
  - [REF-MATOUSEK-1998](../references.md#ref-matousek-1998): Jiří Matoušek. "On the L2-Discrepancy for Anchored Boxes." *Journal of Complexity* 14(4), 527-556, 1998.

Shiue (editors), *Monte Carlo and Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods in Scientific Computing*, Lecture Notes in Statistics 106, Springer, New York, 1995, 299-317.

### EQ-SAMP-OWEN-001 — Owen nested uniform scrambling of a digital net

Digit `k` of each coordinate is XORed with a random bit indexed by
`(k, d_1 ... d_(k-1))`; below the first `m = ceil(log2 n)` digits all bits
are independent uniform.

- Domain: base-2 nets; bits drawn lazily from a seeded stream.
- Meaning: randomized QMC that keeps the net property, makes each point
  uniform, and reduces variance for smooth integrands.
- Code: `sampling.sobol(scramble="owen")`.
- Source: [REF-OWEN-1995].
  - [REF-OWEN-1995](../references.md#ref-owen-1995): Art B. Owen. "Randomly Permuted (t,m,s)-Nets and (t,s)-Sequences." In H. Niederreiter and P. J.-S. Shiue (editors), *Monte Carlo and Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods in Scientific Computing*, Lecture Notes in Statistics 106, Springer, New York, 1995, 299-317.

### EQ-SAMP-MAXIMIN-001 — Morris-Mitchell maximin criterion

`phi_p = (sum_(i<j) d_ij^-p)^(1/p)`, minimized by random within-column swaps
of a Latin hypercube (kept when `phi_p` decreases).

- Domain: distinct points, `p >= 1` (default 15).
- Meaning: space-filling Latin hypercubes with large minimum distances.
- Code: `sampling.maximin_criterion`, `sampling.maximin_latin_hypercube`.
- Source: [REF-MORRIS-MITCHELL-1995].
  - [REF-MORRIS-MITCHELL-1995](../references.md#ref-morris-mitchell-1995): Max D. Morris and Toby J. Mitchell. "Exploratory Designs for Computational Experiments." *Journal of Statistical Planning and Inference* 43(3), 381-402, 1995.
- Registry: [EQ-SAMP-MAXIMIN-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-samp-maximin-001--morris-mitchell-maximin-criterion)

### EQ-SAMP-IC-001 — Iman-Conover rank-correlation induction

Scores `S` (columns: permuted `Phi^-1(i/(n+1))`), `corr(S) = Q Q'`,
`C = P P'`, `T = S (Q^-1)' P'`; reorder each design column to the ranks of
the matching column of `T`.

- Domain: SPD target correlation with unit diagonal, `n >= d + 2`.
- Meaning: rank correlation close to `C` while every marginal (Latin
  stratum) is unchanged.
- Code: `sampling.iman_conover`.
- Source: [REF-IMAN-CONOVER-1982].
  - [REF-IMAN-CONOVER-1982](../references.md#ref-iman-conover-1982): Ronald L. Iman and W. J. Conover. "A Distribution-Free Approach to Inducing Rank Correlation Among Input Variables." *Communications in Statistics - Simulation and Computation* 11(3), 311-334, 1982.
- Registry: [EQ-SAMP-IC-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-samp-ic-001--iman-conover-rank-correlation-induction)
