## `probstat.workflow.chaining`

### EQ-WF-EMPIRICAL-001 — Interpolated empirical law of posterior draws

For distinct sorted points `x_1 < ... < x_m`: `F(x) = (k - 1 + (x - x_k) /
(x_{k+1} - x_k)) / (m - 1)` on `[x_k, x_{k+1}]`, density
`1 / ((m - 1)(x_{k+1} - x_k))`, quantile `Q(p) = x_k + (h - k)(x_{k+1} - x_k)`
with `h = 1 + p (m - 1)`, `k = floor(h)`; mean
`sum_k (x_k + x_{k+1}) / (2 (m - 1))`, second moment
`sum_k (x_k^2 + x_k x_{k+1} + x_{k+1}^2) / (3 (m - 1))`.

- Domain: `m >= 2` distinct finite points; support `[x_1, x_m]`.
- Meaning: the continuous law whose quantile function is the type-7 sample
  quantile ([EQ-STAT-QUANT-001]); each gap carries probability `1/(m - 1)`
  spread uniformly, so moments are exact sums of uniform-segment moments.
- Code: `workflow.EmpiricalMarginal`, `workflow.posterior_joint_input`.
- Source: [REF-HYNDMAN-FAN-1996].
  - [REF-HYNDMAN-FAN-1996](../references.md#ref-hyndman-fan-1996): Rob J. Hyndman and Yanan Fan. "Sample Quantiles in Statistical Packages." *The American Statistician* 50(4), 361-365, 1996.
- Registry: [EQ-WF-EMPIRICAL-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-wf-empirical-001--interpolated-empirical-law-of-posterior-draws)

## `probstat.workflow.mission`

### EQ-WF-MISSION-001 — Expected mission-loss frequency

`nu = n Q(T)`, with `n` missions per year and `Q(T) = 1 - R(T)` the exact
mission unreliability.

- Domain: missions statistically independent and identical; `Q(T)` per
  mission from the exact system model.
- Meaning: expected number of mission losses per year; the rate that drives
  the expected cost of unreliability.
- Code: `workflow.MissionAssessment.cost`, `workflow.MissionAssessment.register`.
- Source: [REF-NASA-PRA-2011], [REF-RAUSAND-BARROS-HOYLAND-2021].

NASA/SP-2011-3421. NASA, Washington, DC, December 2011.

### EQ-WF-MISSION-002 — Probability of at least one loss in a campaign

`P(at least one loss in N missions) = 1 - R(T)^N = -expm1(N log1p(-Q(T)))`.

- Domain: `N = n * horizon` independent identical missions.
- Meaning: campaign-level loss probability, evaluated without cancellation
  when `Q(T)` is small.
- Code: `workflow.MissionAssessment.cost`.
- Source: [REF-NASA-PRA-2011].

### EQ-WF-MISSION-003 — Expected total life-cycle cost including losses

`E[C_total] = PV(E[LCC]) + PV(nu c_loss over the horizon)`.

- Domain: life-cycle totals linear in the cost elements; mission losses at the
  constant expected frequency `nu`; one discount rate.
- Meaning: expected present value of ownership plus the expected cost of
  unreliability; uncertainty in the life-cycle part is shown by the cost-risk
  S-curve, not by this expectation.
- Code: `workflow.MissionAssessment.cost`.
- Source: [REF-NASA-CEH-2015], [REF-RAUSAND-BARROS-HOYLAND-2021].
  - [REF-NASA-CEH-2015](../references.md#ref-nasa-ceh-2015): National Aeronautics and Space Administration. *NASA Cost Estimating Handbook*, Version 4.0. NASA, Washington, DC, February 2015.
  - [REF-RAUSAND-BARROS-HOYLAND-2021](../references.md#ref-rausand-barros-hoyland-2021): Marvin Rausand, Anne Barros, and Arnljot Høyland. *System Reliability Theory: Models, Statistical Methods, and Applications*. 3rd edition. Wiley, 2021.

## `probstat.workflow.study._propagation`

### EQ-WF-SEQ-FRESH-001 — Pilot-sized fresh-sample estimate

A sequential pilot stops at `N = min{n in {n_0, n_0 + h, ...} : r_n <= epsilon}`
(or `N_max`) with `r_n` from [EQ-STAT-STREAM-007] or [EQ-MC-PROB-001]; the
reported estimate is `theta_hat = (1/N) sum_{i=1}^{N} g(X'_i)` with
`X'_1, ..., X'_N` drawn independently of the pilot.

- Domain: any integrable `g`; pilot and final draws on independent streams.
- Meaning: given `N` the fresh sample is a fixed-size Monte Carlo sample, so
  `E[theta_hat | N] = E[g(X)]` and fixed-`n` intervals keep their nominal
  coverage; reusing the pilot instead biases the estimate because stopping
  depends on the values observed (optional stopping). The two-stage idea of
  sizing a sample from a first stage goes back to Stein.
- Code: `workflow.UQStudy.propagate_until` (`final="fresh"`).
- Source: [REF-STEIN-1945] and [REF-ROBERT-CASELLA-2004, Chapters 3-4].
  - [REF-STEIN-1945](../references.md#ref-stein-1945): Charles Stein. "A Two-Sample Test for a Linear Hypothesis Whose Power is Independent of the Variance." *The Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 16(3), 243-258, 1945.
  - [REF-ROBERT-CASELLA-2004](../references.md#ref-robert-casella-2004): Christian P. Robert and George Casella. *Monte Carlo Statistical Methods*. 2nd edition. Springer, 2004. DOI: [10.1007/978-1-4757-4145-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4757-4145-2).
- Registry: [EQ-WF-SEQ-FRESH-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-wf-seq-fresh-001--pilot-sized-fresh-sample-estimate)
