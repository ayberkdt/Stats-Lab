### EQ-FILT-LORENZ-001 — Lorenz-63 system

`dx/dt = sigma (y - x)`, `dy/dt = x (rho - z) - y`, `dz/dt = x y - beta z`;
classical parameters `sigma = 10`, `rho = 28`, `beta = 8/3`. Fixed points
`(0, 0, 0)` and, for `rho > 1`,
`(+-sqrt(beta (rho - 1)), +-sqrt(beta (rho - 1)), rho - 1)`.

- Domain: real `sigma`, `rho`, `beta`; the state-space transition advances
  the system by `substeps` classical Runge-Kutta steps of size `step`
  ([EQ-NUM-ODE-003]); the default step `0.01` is the step of Lorenz (1963),
  who integrated with a second-order scheme.
- Meaning: a truncated convection model whose classical parameters give a
  chaotic attractor; the standard three-variable test of nonlinear filters.
- Code: `filtering.lorenz63_rhs`, `filtering.lorenz63`.
- Source: [REF-LORENZ-1963, Equations 25-27 and Section 7].

### EQ-FILT-NEES-001 — Normalised squared error

`epsilon = e' P^-1 e`, `e = x - x_hat`; for `e ~ N(0, P)` in `n` dimensions
`epsilon ~ chi2(n)`. With an innovation `v` and its covariance `S` the same
form is the NIS, `v' S^-1 v ~ chi2(m)`.

- Domain: `P` symmetric positive definite; Gaussian error under the null.
- Meaning: squared Mahalanobis length of the error, computed through the
  Cholesky factor `P = L L'` as `||L^-1 e||^2`.
- Code: `filtering.nees`, `filtering.nees_series`,
  `filtering.nees_from_filter`, `filtering.nis_series`.

### EQ-FILT-NEES-002 — Monte-Carlo average NEES acceptance interval

`N * mean_i epsilon_(i,k) ~ chi2(N n)`; acceptance interval
`[chi2_(alpha/2)(N n)/N, chi2_(1-alpha/2)(N n)/N]` at each step `k`.

- Domain: `N` independent runs with a consistent filter; the time mean of the
  averages is compared with the same interval (conservative because its
  variance does not exceed the per-step variance).
- Meaning: above the interval the covariance is too small (optimistic), below
  too large (pessimistic).
- Code: `filtering.nees_test`, `filtering.nees_test_from_filters`.
- Source: [REF-BAR-SHALOM-LI-KIRUBARAJAN-2001].

### EQ-FILT-NIS-001 — Time-average NIS acceptance interval

`sum_k v_k' S_k^-1 v_k ~ chi2(D)`, `D = sum_k m_k`; the mean over `T` steps lies

- Domain: one run, independent Gaussian innovations with the stated
  covariances; missing steps are omitted.
- Meaning: a truth-free consistency check of the innovation covariance.

### EQ-FILT-WHITE-001 — Innovation autocorrelation and portmanteau statistic

`rho(tau) = sum_k z_k' z_(k+tau) / sum_k z_k' z_k`, `z_k = L_k^-1 v_k`;

### EQ-FILT-UKF-001 — Scaled unscented transform

`lambda = alpha^2 (n + kappa) - n`; sigma points `chi_0 = m`,
`chi_(i), chi_(n+i) = m +/- sqrt(n + lambda) L_i` with `P = L L'`;
`W_0^m = lambda/(n+lambda)`, `W_0^c = W_0^m + 1 - alpha^2 + beta`,
`W_i^m = W_i^c = 1/(2(n+lambda))`; `mu = sum W_i^m g(chi_i)`,
`Sigma = sum W_i^c (g(chi_i)-mu)(g(chi_i)-mu)'`,
`C = sum W_i^c (chi_i - m)(g(chi_i)-mu)'`.

- Domain: `n + lambda > 0` and positive-semidefinite `P`.
- Meaning: deterministic moment matching; exact mean and covariance for linear
  `g`, exact mean for quadratic `g`.
- Code: `filtering.UnscentedWeights`, `filtering.unscented_transform`.

= sum_(i,j) w_i w_j N(h(i - j); 0, 2 s^2) - 2 sum_i w_i N(i h; 0, 1 + s^2) + N(0; 0, 2)`,

### EQ-FILT-GMSPLIT-002 — Multivariate split along a direction

`sum_i w_i m_i = m` and `sum_i w_i [C + (m_i - m)(m_i - m)'] = P`.

- Domain: `P` positive definite, `d != 0`.
- Meaning: the split acts on the unit vector `P^-1/2 d` in whitened

### EQ-FILT-BAYES-001 — Sequential Bayesian filtering recursion

  current state.
- Meaning: every filter in the library is an exact or approximate

### EQ-FILT-EM-001 — EM M-step for a linear-Gaussian model

With smoothed `m^s_k, P^s_k` and gains `G_k`:
`P^s_(k,k-1) = P^s_k G_(k-1)'`,
`Sigma = T^-1 sum_(k=1..T) (P^s_k + m^s_k m^s_k')`,
`Phi = T^-1 sum_(k=1..T) (P^s_(k-1) + m^s_(k-1) m^s_(k-1)')`,
`C = T^-1 sum_(k=1..T) (P^s_(k,k-1) + m^s_k m^s_(k-1)')`; over the `T_o`

`B = T_o^-1 sum y_k m^s_k'`, `D = T_o^-1 sum y_k y_k'`. Updates:
`F = C Phi^-1`, `Q = Sigma - C F' - F C' + F Phi F'`, `H = B Sigma_o^-1`,
`R = D - H B' - B H' + H Sigma_o H'`, `m_0 = m^s_0`, `P_0 = P^s_0`.

- Domain: [EQ-FILT-SSM-001] without controls; positive-definite `Phi` and
  `Sigma_o`.
- Meaning: each update maximizes the expected complete-data log-likelihood
  given the blocks held fixed, so the exact log-likelihood never decreases.
- Code: `filtering.linear_gaussian_em`, `filtering.smoothed_lag_one_covariances`.

`W_(k|k+1)^i ∝ W_k^i N(x~_(k+1); f(x_k^i) + B u_(k+1), Q)` and

- Domain: stored filter clouds `{x_k^i, W_k^i}` for `k = 0..T`;
  positive-definite `Q`.
- Meaning: each drawn trajectory is approximately distributed as
  `p(x_0:T | y_1:T)`; the cost is `O(N T)` per trajectory.
- Code: `filtering.ffbs_particle_smoother`.
- Source: [REF-GODSILL-DOUCET-WEST-2004] and
  [REF-DOUCET-JOHANSEN-2011, Section 5.2].
  - [REF-GODSILL-DOUCET-WEST-2004](../references.md#ref-godsill-doucet-west-2004): Simon J. Godsill, Arnaud Doucet, and Mike West. "Monte Carlo Smoothing for Nonlinear Time Series." *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 99(465), 156-168, 2004. Forward filtering, backward simulation of whole state trajectories from stored particle-filter output. DOI: [10.1198/016214504000000151](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214504000000151).
  - [REF-DOUCET-JOHANSEN-2011](../references.md#ref-doucet-johansen-2011): Arnaud Doucet and Adam M. Johansen. "A Tutorial on Particle Filtering and Smoothing: Fifteen Years Later." In D. Crisan and B. Rozovskii (eds.), *The Oxford Handbook of Nonlinear Filtering*. Oxford University Press, 2011.

## `probstat.filtering.state_space`

### EQ-FILT-SSM-001 — Linear-Gaussian state-space model

`x_k = F x_(k-1) + B u_k + w_k`, `w_k ~ N(0,Q)`; `y_k = H x_k + v_k`,
`v_k ~ N(0,R)`; `x_0 ~ N(m_0,P_0)`, with `w`, `v`, and `x_0` mutually
independent.

- Domain: `Q` and `P_0` symmetric positive semidefinite, `R` symmetric
  positive definite, conformable `F`, `B`, `H`; observations `y_1..y_T`
  follow the transitions.
- Meaning: `Q` is process (dynamics) uncertainty that inflates predictions;
  `R` is measurement uncertainty that limits how far one observation can move
  the estimate.
- Code: `filtering.LinearGaussianStateSpace`, `filtering.simulate`.
- Source: [REF-KALMAN-1960] and [REF-SARKKA-2013, Chapter 4].
  - [REF-KALMAN-1960](../references.md#ref-kalman-1960): R. E. Kalman. "A New Approach to Linear Filtering and Prediction Problems." *Transactions of the ASME, Journal of Basic Engineering* 82(1), 35-45, 1960. DOI: [10.1115/1.3662552](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.3662552).
  - [REF-SARKKA-2013](../references.md#ref-sarkka-2013): Simo Särkkä. *Bayesian Filtering and Smoothing*. Institute of Mathematical Statistics Textbooks. Cambridge University Press, 2013.
- Registry: [EQ-FILT-SSM-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-filt-ssm-001--linear-gaussian-state-space-model)

### EQ-FILT-SSM-002 — Nonlinear additive-noise state-space model

`x_k = f(x_(k-1)) + w_k`, `y_k = h(x_k) + v_k`, with Gaussian `w_k`, `v_k`,
and `x_0` as in [EQ-FILT-SSM-001].

- Domain: measurable `f` and `h`; differentiable where an extended filter
  linearizes them.
- Meaning: the common model consumed by extended, unscented, ensemble, and
  particle methods.
- Code: `filtering.NonlinearStateSpace`, `filtering.simulate`,
  `data_assimilation.AdditiveGaussianModel`.
- Source: [REF-SARKKA-2013, Chapters 4-5].
  - [REF-SARKKA-2013](../references.md#ref-sarkka-2013): Simo Särkkä. *Bayesian Filtering and Smoothing*. Institute of Mathematical Statistics Textbooks. Cambridge University Press, 2013.
- Also names: [`data_assimilation`](data_assimilation.md)
- Registry: [EQ-FILT-SSM-002](../equation-registry.md#eq-filt-ssm-002--nonlinear-additive-noise-state-space-model)
