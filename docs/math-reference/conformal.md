## `probstat.conformal.adaptive`

### EQ-CONF-ACI-001 — Adaptive conformal level update

`err_t = 1{S_t(X_t, Y_t) > Q_t(1 - alpha_t)}`,
`alpha_(t+1) = alpha_t + gamma (alpha - err_t)`, with `alpha_1` in `[0, 1]`.

- Domain: target miscoverage `alpha` in `(0, 1)`, step `gamma >= 0`, any
  sequence of scores (no exchangeability assumption).
- Meaning: an online step on the single parameter that absorbs distribution
  shift; a miss lowers `alpha_t` (wider sets), a cover raises it.
- Code: `conformal.AdaptiveConformalPredictor.update`,
  `conformal.adaptive_conformal_intervals`.
- Source: [REF-GIBBS-CANDES-2021, Equation 2].

In *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 34* (NeurIPS 2021), 2021. Preprint arXiv:2106.00170v3, 2021. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170).

### EQ-CONF-ACI-002 — Adaptive conformal score threshold and interval

For a score history `H` of size `m`: `Q_t(1 - alpha_t) = -inf` if
`alpha_t >= 1`; `+inf` if `alpha_t <= 0`, `m = 0`, or
`k = ceil((1 - alpha_t)(m + 1)) > m`; otherwise the `k`-th smallest score.
The interval is `yhat_t +/- Q_t(1 - alpha_t) s_t`, with score
`S_t = |y_t - yhat_t| / s_t`.

- Domain: non-negative scores; positive spreads `s_t` (`s_t = 1` for
  absolute residuals); optional sliding window over `H`.
- Meaning: the split conformal quantile at the adapted level; the infinite
  thresholds at the boundary levels are what keep `alpha_t` inside
  `[-gamma, 1 + gamma]`.
- Code: `conformal.AdaptiveConformalPredictor.radius`,
  `conformal.AdaptiveConformalPredictor.interval`.
- Source: [REF-GIBBS-CANDES-2021, Equation 1 and Section 4.1] with the
  finite-sample index of [EQ-CONF-001].
  - [REF-GIBBS-CANDES-2021](../references.md#ref-gibbs-candes-2021): Isaac Gibbs and Emmanuel J. Candès. "Adaptive Conformal Inference Under Distribution Shift." In *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 34* (NeurIPS 2021), 2021. Preprint arXiv:2106.00170v3, 2021. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170).
- Registry: [EQ-CONF-ACI-002](../equation-registry.md#eq-conf-aci-002--adaptive-conformal-score-threshold-and-interval)

### EQ-CONF-ACI-003 — Long-run miscoverage bound

`|(1/T) sum_(t=1..T) err_t - alpha| <= (max(alpha_1, 1 - alpha_1) + gamma) / (gamma T)`
for every `T`, since `alpha_t` stays in `[-gamma, 1 + gamma]`.

- Domain: `gamma > 0`; holds deterministically for every data sequence.
- Meaning: the empirical miscoverage frequency converges to `alpha` at rate
  `1/T` without any assumption on the data-generating process; it says
  nothing about conditional coverage at a given time.
- Code: `conformal.AdaptiveConformalPredictor.long_run_bound`,
  `conformal.AdaptiveConformalResult`.
- Source: [REF-GIBBS-CANDES-2021, Lemma 4.1 and Proposition 4.1].
  - [REF-GIBBS-CANDES-2021](../references.md#ref-gibbs-candes-2021): Isaac Gibbs and Emmanuel J. Candès. "Adaptive Conformal Inference Under Distribution Shift." In *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 34* (NeurIPS 2021), 2021. Preprint arXiv:2106.00170v3, 2021. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170).

## `probstat.conformal.quantile`

### EQ-CONF-001 — Finite-sample conformal quantile

`k = ceil((1 - alpha)(n + 1))`, `q_hat = s_(k)` (the `k`-th smallest of `n`
calibration scores), `q_hat = +inf` when `k = n + 1`; equivalently the
empirical `(1 - alpha)(n + 1)/n` quantile.

- Domain: `0 < alpha < 1`, `n >= 1`; computed exactly from the decimal text of
  `alpha`.
- Meaning: the `(n + 1)/n` inflation makes room for the exchangeable test
  point; `alpha < 1/(n + 1)` forces infinite sets.
- Code: `conformal.quantile.conformal_rank`,
  `conformal.quantile.conformal_quantile`.
- Source: [REF-VOVK-GAMMERMAN-SHAFER-2005], [REF-LEI-ETAL-2018],

### EQ-CONF-005 — Split conformal marginal coverage

`1 - alpha <= k / (n + 1) <= P(Y_{n+1} in C(X_{n+1}))`, with equality
`P = k/(n + 1) < 1 - alpha + 1/(n + 1)` when scores are almost surely distinct.

- Domain: exchangeable calibration and test points; the probability is over
  both the calibration set and the test point (marginal, not conditional on
  `x` or on the calibration set).
- Meaning: the exact target against which empirical coverage is checked; a
  coverage count on held-out data is binomial and its Clopper-Pearson interval
  shows how precisely coverage has been measured.
- Code: `conformal.quantile.split_conformal_coverage`,
  `conformal.coverage.empirical_coverage`,
  `conformal.regression.SplitConformalRegressor.guaranteed_coverage`.
- Source: [REF-LEI-ETAL-2018], [REF-ANGELOPOULOS-BATES-2023].
  - [REF-LEI-ETAL-2018](../references.md#ref-lei-etal-2018): Jing Lei, Max G'Sell, Alessandro Rinaldo, Ryan J. Tibshirani, and Larry Wasserman. "Distribution-Free Predictive Inference for Regression." *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 113(523), 1094-1111, 2018.

## `probstat.conformal.regression`

### EQ-CONF-002 — Split conformal regression intervals

Absolute score `s_i = |y_i - mu(x_i)|`, interval `mu(x) +/- q_hat`;
normalized (locally weighted) score `s_i = |y_i - mu(x_i)| / sigma(x_i)`,
interval `mu(x) +/- q_hat sigma(x)`, with `mu` and `sigma > 0` fixed before
seeing the calibration data.

- Domain: calibration and test pairs exchangeable; `mu`, `sigma` independent
  of the calibration set.
- Meaning: wraps any predictor (including a surrogate of a probabilistic
  model) with marginally valid error bars; normalization adapts the width to
  `sigma(x)` without changing the guarantee.
- Code: `conformal.regression.split_conformal_regression`,
  `conformal.regression.SplitConformalRegressor.interval`,
  `conformal.surrogate.conformalize_surrogate`.
- Source: [REF-LEI-ETAL-2018], [REF-VOVK-GAMMERMAN-SHAFER-2005].
  - [REF-LEI-ETAL-2018](../references.md#ref-lei-etal-2018): Jing Lei, Max G'Sell, Alessandro Rinaldo, Ryan J. Tibshirani, and Larry Wasserman. "Distribution-Free Predictive Inference for Regression." *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 113(523), 1094-1111, 2018.
  - [REF-VOVK-GAMMERMAN-SHAFER-2005](../references.md#ref-vovk-gammerman-shafer-2005): Vladimir Vovk, Alex Gammerman, and Glenn Shafer. *Algorithmic Learning in a Random World*. Springer, 2005.

### EQ-CONF-003 — Conformalized quantile regression

`E_i = max(q_lo(x_i) - y_i, y_i - q_hi(x_i))`, interval
`[q_lo(x) - q_hat, q_hi(x) + q_hat]` with `q_hat` from EQ-CONF-001 applied to
the `E_i`.

- Domain: as EQ-CONF-002; `q_lo`, `q_hi` any functions fitted without the
  calibration data.
- Meaning: shifts estimated conditional-quantile bands outward (or inward when
  `q_hat < 0`) until they are marginally valid, keeping their adaptive width.
- Code: `conformal.regression.conformalized_quantile_regression`,
  `conformal.regression.ConformalizedQuantileRegressor.interval`.
- Source: [REF-ROMANO-PATTERSON-CANDES-2019].
  - [REF-ROMANO-PATTERSON-CANDES-2019](../references.md#ref-romano-patterson-candes-2019): Yaniv Romano, Evan Patterson, and Emmanuel J. Candès. "Conformalized Quantile Regression." In *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32 (NeurIPS 2019)*, 2019.
- Registry: [EQ-CONF-003](../equation-registry.md#eq-conf-003--conformalized-quantile-regression)

### EQ-CONF-004 — Jackknife+ interval

`R_i = |y_i - mu_{-i}(x_i)|`; lower limit the `floor(alpha (n + 1))`-th
smallest of `mu_{-i}(x) - R_i` (`-inf` if that index is 0), upper limit the
`ceil((1 - alpha)(n + 1))`-th smallest of `mu_{-i}(x) + R_i` (`+inf` if it
exceeds `n`).

- Domain: exchangeable training and test data; a fitting algorithm that is
  symmetric in its training points.
- Meaning: uses every point for both fitting and calibration at the cost of
  `n` refits; marginal coverage is at least `1 - 2 alpha` and typically near
  `1 - alpha`.
- Code: `conformal.regression.jackknife_plus_regression`,
  `conformal.regression.JackknifePlusRegressor.interval`,
  `conformal.quantile.lower_conformal_rank`.
- Source: [REF-BARBER-ETAL-2021].
  - [REF-BARBER-ETAL-2021](../references.md#ref-barber-etal-2021): Rina Foygel Barber, Emmanuel J. Candès, Aaditya Ramdas, and Ryan J. Tibshirani. "Predictive Inference with the Jackknife+." *The Annals of Statistics* 49(1), 486-507, 2021.
