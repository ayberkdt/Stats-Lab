## `probstat.propagation.forward`

### EQ-PROP-EXP-001 — Expectation integral

`E[g(X)] = integral g(x) f_X(x) dx`.

- Domain: measurable transform and density with an absolutely integrable
  product; the implemented rule integrates only explicitly supplied finite
  bounds.
- Meaning: deterministic uncertainty propagation of a scalar transform through
  its input density.
- Code: `propagation.expectation_by_quadrature`.
- Source: [REF-SMITH-UQ-2024, Chapter 3].
  - [REF-SMITH-UQ-2024](../references.md#ref-smith-uq-2024): Ralph C. Smith. *Uncertainty Quantification: Theory, Implementation, and Applications*. 2nd edition. Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, 2024. DOI: [10.1137/1.9781611977844](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611977844).
- Registry: [EQ-PROP-EXP-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-prop-exp-001--expectation-integral)

### EQ-PROP-THRESH-001 — Strict exceedance probability

`P(Y>S)=E[I(Y>S)]`.

- Domain: finite threshold `S` and propagated finite output samples.
- Meaning: turns a decision threshold into a Bernoulli Monte Carlo estimator;
  equality is not counted as exceedance.
- Code: `propagation.propagate_samples` and
  `monte_carlo.estimate_probability`.
- Source: [REF-SMITH-UQ-2024, Chapter 4].
  - [REF-SMITH-UQ-2024](../references.md#ref-smith-uq-2024): Ralph C. Smith. *Uncertainty Quantification: Theory, Implementation, and Applications*. 2nd edition. Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, 2024. DOI: [10.1137/1.9781611977844](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611977844).
- Also names: [`monte_carlo`](monte_carlo.md)

## `probstat.propagation.mixed`

### EQ-PROP-MIXED-001 — Nested (double-loop) propagation

For outer epistemic values `e_j` (Latin hypercube over the epistemic
dimensions, plus interval corners and optional compass-search points):
`F_j(y) = (1/n) sum_i 1{g(x_i, e_j) <= y}` with `x_i` the aleatory inner
sample (common to every `j` with common random numbers); statistics
`s(e_j)` = mean, `P(Y > t)`, type-7 quantiles; bounds
`[min_j s(e_j), max_j s(e_j)]`.

- Domain: aleatory and epistemic inputs independent; at least one of each.
- Meaning: separates variability (inner CDF) from lack of knowledge (outer
  spread). The bounds over a finite design are inner approximations of the
  true bounds (exact at the corners for responses monotone in each interval
  input); cost `(outer points) x n` model evaluations.
- Code: `propagation.nested_propagation`.
- Source: [REF-ROY-OBERKAMPF-2011], [REF-OBERKAMPF-ROY-2010].
  - [REF-ROY-OBERKAMPF-2011](../references.md#ref-roy-oberkampf-2011): Christopher J. Roy and William L. Oberkampf. "A Comprehensive Framework for Verification, Validation, and Uncertainty Quantification in Scientific Computing." *Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering* 200(25-28), 2131-2144, 2011.
  - [REF-OBERKAMPF-ROY-2010](../references.md#ref-oberkampf-roy-2010): William L. Oberkampf and Christopher J. Roy. *Verification and Validation in Scientific Computing*. Cambridge University Press, 2010.
- Registry: [EQ-PROP-MIXED-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-prop-mixed-001--nested-double-loop-propagation)

### EQ-PROP-PBOX-001 — Probability box from a family of CDFs

`F_low(y) = min_j F_j(y)`, `F_up(y) = max_j F_j(y)`;
`P(Y > t)` in `[1 - F_up(t), 1 - F_low(t)]`.

- Domain: the outer points of [EQ-PROP-MIXED-001].
- Meaning: the pointwise envelope of the output CDFs (a p-box); for
  `Y = X + E`, `E` in `[a, b]`, it is `[Phi(y - b), Phi(y - a)]`.
- Code: `propagation.ProbabilityBox.bounds`,
  `propagation.ProbabilityBox.exceedance_bounds`.
- Source: [REF-FERSON-ET-AL-2003].
  - [REF-FERSON-ET-AL-2003](../references.md#ref-ferson-et-al-2003): Scott Ferson, Vladik Kreinovich, Lev Ginzburg, Davis S. Myers, and Kari Sentz. *Constructing Probability Boxes and Dempster-Shafer Structures*. Sandia National Laboratories Report SAND2002-4015, 2003.
- Registry: [EQ-PROP-PBOX-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-prop-pbox-001--probability-box-from-a-family-of-cdfs)

## `probstat.propagation.study`

### EQ-PROP-QCI-001 — Distribution-free order-statistic interval for a quantile

`P(Y_(l) <= xi_p <= Y_(u)) = B(u - 1) - B(l - 1)` with `B` the
`Binomial(n, p)` CDF; `l = max{k >= 1 : B(k - 1) <= alpha/2}`,
`u = min{k <= n : B(k - 1) >= 1 - alpha/2}`.

- Domain: `n` independent draws of a continuous law, `0 < p < 1`; undefined
  (error) when no rank pair reaches the confidence.
- Meaning: an exact, law-free confidence interval for the `p`-quantile whose
  coverage is at least `1 - alpha`, with each tail at most `alpha/2`; for LHS
  or quasi-random samples it is a conservative reference only.
- Code: `propagation.order_statistic_interval`,
  `propagation.OutputDistribution.quantile_interval`.
- Source: [REF-DAVID-NAGARAJA-2003, Section 7.1].
  - [REF-DAVID-NAGARAJA-2003](../references.md#ref-david-nagaraja-2003): H. A. David and H. N. Nagaraja. *Order Statistics*. Third edition. Wiley Series in Probability and Statistics. John Wiley & Sons, Hoboken, NJ, 2003.
- Registry: [EQ-PROP-QCI-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-prop-qci-001--distribution-free-order-statistic-interval-for-a-quantile)

### EQ-PROP-RQMC-CI-001 — Student-t interval over randomized replicates

`theta_hat = (1/r) sum_k theta_k`, `s_r^2 = sum_k (theta_k - theta_hat)^2 /
(r - 1)`, interval `theta_hat +- t_{1 - alpha/2, r - 1} s_r / sqrt(r)`.

- Domain: `r >= 2` independent randomizations (scrambles, shifts, or
  independent Monte Carlo batches) of one design; `theta_k` the replicate
  mean or exceedance fraction.
- Meaning: the honest error statement of randomized quasi-Monte Carlo; the
  replicate estimates are i.i.d. and unbiased, and the t quantile is accurate
  when their distribution is near normal.
- Code: `propagation.replicate_interval`,
  `propagation.OutputDistribution.mean_interval`,
  `propagation.OutputDistribution.exceedance_interval`.
- Source: [REF-L-ECUYER-2018] and [REF-OWEN-2013, Chapter 17].
  - [REF-L-ECUYER-2018](../references.md#ref-l-ecuyer-2018): Pierre L'Ecuyer. "Randomized Quasi-Monte Carlo: An Introduction for Practitioners." In *Monte Carlo and Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods (MCQMC 2016)*, Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics 241, 29-52, 2018.
  - [REF-OWEN-2013](../references.md#ref-owen-2013): Art B. Owen. *Monte Carlo Theory, Methods and Examples*. 2013. [https://artowen.su.domains/mc/](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/).
- Registry: [EQ-PROP-RQMC-CI-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-prop-rqmc-ci-001--student-t-interval-over-randomized-replicates)

### EQ-PROP-VARCI-001 — Chi-square interval for a normal variance

`[(n - 1) s^2 / chi2_{1 - alpha/2, n - 1}, (n - 1) s^2 / chi2_{alpha/2, n - 1}]`.

- Domain: `n >= 2` independent normal observations with sample variance
  `s^2`.
- Meaning: exact for normal data because `(n - 1) s^2 / sigma^2 ~
  chi2_{n - 1}`; not robust: for non-normal data its coverage depends on the
  kurtosis even as `n` grows, so the percentile bootstrap
  ([EQ-INF-BOOT-002]) is offered as the alternative.
- Code: `propagation.OutputDistribution.variance_interval`.
- Source: [REF-CASELLA-BERGER-2002, Chapter 9].
  - [REF-CASELLA-BERGER-2002](../references.md#ref-casella-berger-2002): George Casella and Roger L. Berger. *Statistical Inference*. 2nd edition. Duxbury, 2002.
- Registry: [EQ-PROP-VARCI-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-prop-varci-001--chi-square-interval-for-a-normal-variance)
