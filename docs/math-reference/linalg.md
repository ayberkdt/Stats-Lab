## `probstat.linalg.backend`

### EQ-LINALG-010 — Explicitly shifted Cholesky factorization

`A + delta I = L L'` with a caller-supplied shift `delta >= 0`; `delta = 0`
is the plain factorization [EQ-LINALG-001].

- Domain: finite symmetric `A` whose shift `A + delta I` is positive
  definite, i.e. `delta > -lambda_min(A)`; otherwise the factorization fails
  and the failure is reported, never repaired by enlarging `delta`.
- Meaning: the jitter policy of every dense backend. The shift is part of
  the caller's model (a nugget or noise variance), so it is always visible
  in the arguments and recorded on the factorization.
- Code: `linalg.DenseBackend.cholesky`, `linalg.DenseBackend.factorize`,
  `linalg.CholeskyFactorization.jitter`.
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 4.2] and
  [REF-HIGHAM-2002, Chapter 10].
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
  - [REF-HIGHAM-2002](../references.md#ref-higham-2002): Nicholas J. Higham. *Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms*. 2nd edition. Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM), 2002.
- Registry: [EQ-LINALG-010](../equation-registry.md#eq-linalg-010--explicitly-shifted-cholesky-factorization)

### EQ-LINALG-012 — Agreement bound between backward-stable backends

With unit roundoff `u = 2^-53` and `gamma_k = k u / (1 - k u)`:

- Cholesky residual, componentwise: `|A - L L'| <= gamma_(n+1) |L||L'|`.
- Factor agreement: `||L_1 - L_2||_F / ||L||_2 <= 2 kappa_2(A) gamma_(n+1)
  tr(A) / ||A||_2`.
- SPD solve agreement: `||x_1 - x_2|| / ||x|| <= 2 kappa_2(A)
  gamma_(3n+1) tr(A) / ||A||_2`.
- Log-determinant agreement: `|log det_1 - log det_2| <= n` times the
  factor bound.
- Products, componentwise: `|fl_1(AB) - fl_2(AB)| <= (gamma_k + 2u) |A||B|`
  for inner dimension `k`.
- Eigenvalues (Weyl): `|lambda_i,1 - lambda_i,2| <= ||E_1||_2 + ||E_2||_2`,
  each `||E||` bounded by the method's backward error.

- Domain: two implementations that are each backward stable in the sense
  of the cited analyses; first order in `u`.
- Meaning: each result is exact for a matrix within `gamma_k |L||L'|` of
  `A`, so `|| |L||L'| ||_F <= ||L||_F^2 = tr(A)` converts the componentwise
  backward error to `gamma_k tr(A)`; perturbation theory multiplies it by at
  most `kappa_2(A)` in the forward error, and two results differ by at most
  the sum of their errors. The bound is a test tolerance, not an error
  estimate: observed differences are many orders of magnitude smaller.
- Code: `tests/test_linalg_backend.py` (reference comparisons of
  `linalg.NumPyBackend` against `linalg.PythonBackend`).
- Source: [REF-HIGHAM-2002, Chapters 3, 7, 8, and 10] and
  [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 8.1].
  - [REF-HIGHAM-2002](../references.md#ref-higham-2002): Nicholas J. Higham. *Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms*. 2nd edition. Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM), 2002.
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
- Registry: [EQ-LINALG-012](../equation-registry.md#eq-linalg-012--agreement-bound-between-backward-stable-backends)

## `probstat.linalg.dense`

### EQ-LINALG-001 — Cholesky factorization

`A=LL'` for symmetric positive-definite `A` and lower-triangular `L` with a
positive diagonal.

- Domain: finite symmetric positive-definite square matrix.
- Meaning: exposes determinant, solves, and Gaussian transforms without an
  explicit inverse.
- Code: `linalg.cholesky`, `linalg.cholesky_semidefinite`, and every
  Gaussian consumer (`multivariate`, `inference`, `filtering`).
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 4.2].
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
- Registry: [EQ-LINALG-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-linalg-001--cholesky-factorization)

### EQ-LINALG-002 — Forward substitution

`x_i=(b_i-sum_(j<i) L_ij x_j)/L_ii`.

- Domain: lower-triangular `L` with nonzero diagonal and matching finite `b`.
- Meaning: solves `Lx=b` one component at a time.
- Code: `linalg.solve_lower` and `linalg.cholesky_solve`.
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 3.1].
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
- Registry: [EQ-LINALG-002](../equation-registry.md#eq-linalg-002--forward-substitution)

### EQ-LINALG-003 — Back substitution

`x_i=(b_i-sum_(j>i) U_ij x_j)/U_ii`, evaluated from `i=n` down to `1`.

- Domain: upper-triangular `U` with nonzero diagonal and matching finite `b`.
- Meaning: solves `Ux=b`; paired with [EQ-LINALG-002] it solves `LL'x=b`.
- Code: `linalg.solve_upper`, `linalg.cholesky_solve`, `linalg.solve_spd`.
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 3.1].
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
- Registry: [EQ-LINALG-003](../equation-registry.md#eq-linalg-003--back-substitution)

### EQ-LINALG-004 — Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting

`PA=LU`, where each elimination step swaps in the row with the largest
remaining pivot magnitude before subtracting multiples of it.

- Domain: square finite non-singular `A`; a pivot below `1e-14 max|A_ij|` is
  reported as singular rather than perturbed.
- Meaning: general dense solve used where symmetry or definiteness is not
  guaranteed, such as Newton steps on nonsymmetric Jacobians.
- Code: `linalg.lu_solve`.
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 3.4].
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
- Registry: [EQ-LINALG-004](../equation-registry.md#eq-linalg-004--gaussian-elimination-with-partial-pivoting)

### EQ-LINALG-005 — Cyclic Jacobi eigenvalue iteration

`A_(k+1)=J_k' A_k J_k`, where each plane rotation `J_k(p,q,theta)` zeroes
`A_pq`; `V=prod J_k` accumulates the eigenvectors.

- Domain: real symmetric finite `A`; iteration stops when the off-diagonal
  Frobenius norm falls below a relative tolerance.
- Meaning: returns `A=V diag(lambda) V'` with orthonormal `V`, used for
  curvature analysis (SORM), spectral conditioning, and covariance geometry.
- Code: `linalg.symmetric_eigen`.
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 8.5].
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.

### EQ-LINALG-006 — Log-determinant from a Cholesky factor

### EQ-LINALG-007 — Linear covariance propagation

`Cov(HX)=H Sigma H'` for a linear map `H` and `Cov(X)=Sigma`.

- Domain: finite conformable `H` and symmetric `Sigma`; the result is
  symmetrized to remove rounding asymmetry.
- Meaning: exact second-moment propagation through linear maps; the basis of
  Gaussian predictive covariance and Kalman prediction.
- Code: `linalg.sandwich`.
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 1.1] and

### EQ-LINALG-008 — Determinant from a partial-pivot LU factorization

`PA=LU` with unit lower-triangular `L` gives `det A = (-1)^s prod_i U_ii` and
`log|det A| = sum_i log|U_ii|`, where `s` is the number of row interchanges.

- Domain: square finite `A`. Only an exactly zero pivot column is reported as
  singular (determinant `0`, log-determinant error), so uniformly tiny but
  non-singular matrices keep their determinant.
- Meaning: volume scaling of the linear map `x -> Ax`; the log form is the
  Jacobian term of a change of variables and does not overflow.
- Code: `linalg.determinant`, `linalg.log_abs_determinant`,
  `multivariate.TransformedDensity.log_abs_det_jacobian`.
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Section 3.4] and
  [REF-STRANG-2016, Chapter 5].
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
  - [REF-STRANG-2016](../references.md#ref-strang-2016): Gilbert Strang. *Introduction to Linear Algebra*. 5th edition. Wellesley-Cambridge Press, 2016.
- Also names: [`multivariate`](multivariate.md)
- Registry: [EQ-LINALG-008](../equation-registry.md#eq-linalg-008--determinant-from-a-partial-pivot-lu-factorization)

### EQ-LINALG-011 — Inverse from a Cholesky factor

`A^-1 = L^-T L^-1` for `A = L L'`, with `L^-1` obtained by forward
substitution [EQ-LINALG-002] on the columns of `I` (equivalently, column
`j` of `A^-1` solves `L L' x = e_j`); the result is symmetrized as
`(X + X') / 2`.

- Domain: `L` lower triangular with a positive diagonal.
- Meaning: every entry of `A^-1` from one existing factorization, as needed
  by the Gaussian-process likelihood gradient, leave-one-out diagonal, and
  condition number.
- Code: `linalg.inverse_spd`, `linalg.CholeskyFactorization.inverse`,
  `linalg.DenseBackend.inverse_spd`.
- Source: [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Sections 3.1 and 4.2] and
  [REF-HIGHAM-2002, Chapter 14].
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
  - [REF-HIGHAM-2002](../references.md#ref-higham-2002): Nicholas J. Higham. *Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms*. 2nd edition. Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM), 2002.

## `probstat.linalg.qr`

### EQ-LINALG-009 — Householder QR factorization and least squares

`H_k = I - 2 v_k v_k'` with `v_k` proportional to `x - alpha e_1`,
`alpha = -sign(x_1) ||x||`, applied to the trailing part of column `k`; then
`A = Q R` with `Q = H_0 ... H_(p-1)` (thin, `n x p`) and upper-triangular
`R`. The least-squares solution is `x = R^-1 Q'b`, `(A'A)^-1 = R^-1 R^-T`, and
the leverages are `h_i = sum_j Q_ij^2`.

- Domain: finite `n x p` matrix with `n >= p`; without pivoting, column `j`
  is flagged rank deficient when `|R_jj| <= tol ||a_j||` (default
  `tol = 1e-10`), and the solve then refuses instead of regularizing.
- Meaning: least squares without forming `A'A`, so the solve sees `kappa(A)`
  rather than `kappa(A)^2`.
- Code: `linalg.householder_qr`, `linalg.HouseholderQR.solve`,
  `linalg.HouseholderQR.gram_inverse`, `linalg.HouseholderQR.hat_diagonal`.
- Source: [REF-HOUSEHOLDER-1958], [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013, Sections 5.1-5.3].
  - [REF-HOUSEHOLDER-1958](../references.md#ref-householder-1958): A. S. Householder. "Unitary Triangularization of a Nonsymmetric Matrix." *Journal of the ACM* 5(4), 339-342, 1958.
  - [REF-GOLUB-VANLOAN-2013](../references.md#ref-golub-vanloan-2013): Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan. *Matrix Computations*. 4th edition. Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
- Registry: [EQ-LINALG-009](../equation-registry.md#eq-linalg-009--householder-qr-factorization-and-least-squares)
