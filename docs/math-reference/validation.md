## `probstat.validation.metrics`

### EQ-VAL-AREA-001 — Area validation metric

`d(F, S_n) = integral_{-inf}^{inf} |F(y) - S_n(y)| dy`, where `F` is the
predictive CDF and `S_n` the empirical CDF of the observations. For two step
CDFs with merged breakpoints `z_1 < ... < z_K`,
`d = sum_k |F(z_k) - S_n(z_k)| (z_{k+1} - z_k)` exactly.

- Domain: non-empty finite predictive samples and observations; both CDFs are
  right-continuous step functions.
- Meaning: mismatch between prediction and data in QoI units. If either law is
  a point mass at `c`, `d` equals the mean absolute distance of the other
  sample from `c`; `d = 0` only for identical distributions.
- Code: `validation.metrics.area_metric`.
- Source: [REF-FERSON-OBERKAMPF-GINZBURG-2008], [REF-OBERKAMPF-ROY-2025,
  Chapter 12].

### EQ-VAL-BF-001 — Validation Bayes factor against a declared alternative

`p_M(y) = (1/S) sum_s N(y; y_s, h^2)`, `log B = sum_i log p_M(y_i) -

### EQ-VAL-STACK-001 — Log-score stacking of predictive distributions

`w* = argmax_(w in simplex) (1/n) sum_(i=1..n) log sum_(k=1..K) w_k p_k,-i(y_i)`,
computed by `w_k <- w_k g_k`, `g_k = (1/n) sum_i p_ik / sum_j w_j p_ij`,
stopped when `max_k g_k - 1 <= tol`, which bounds `f(w*) - f(w)` by
concavity.

- Domain: pointwise out-of-sample (leave-one-out or held-out validation)
  predictive densities `p_ik >= 0`, each row positive for some model.
- Meaning: the weighted mixture of the models' predictive distributions
  with the best expected log score; it keeps several models when each is
  right on part of the data, unlike Bayesian model averaging.
- Code: `validation.stacking_weights`.
- Source: [REF-YAO-ETAL-2018, Section 2.1, Equation 2.2] (objective),
  [REF-MCLACHLAN-PEEL-2000] (EM update of mixing proportions), and

### EQ-VAL-STACK-002 — Pseudo-BMA and pseudo-BMA+ weights

`w_k = exp(elpd_k) / sum_j exp(elpd_j)`, `elpd_k = sum_i log p_k,-i(y_i)`;
pseudo-BMA+: `a ~ Dirichlet(1, ..., 1)`, `z_k = n sum_i a_i log p_k,-i(y_i)`,
`w_k = mean over B draws of exp(z_k) / sum_j exp(z_j)`.

- Domain: as [EQ-VAL-STACK-001]; `B >= 1` Bayesian-bootstrap draws.
- Meaning: AIC-type weights from the estimated expected log predictive
  density; the Bayesian bootstrap accounts for the uncertainty of the elpd
  estimate and moves weights away from 0 and 1.

### EQ-VAL-SCORE-CRPS-002 — Ensemble CRPS by sorting

### EQ-VAL-SCORE-BSDEC-001 — Murphy decomposition

For bins `k` with `n_k` forecasts, mean forecast `fbar_k`, event frequency
`obar_k` and climatology `obar`:
`REL = (1/N) sum n_k (fbar_k - obar_k)^2`, `RES = (1/N) sum n_k (obar_k - obar)^2`,
`UNC = obar (1 - obar)`, and `BS = REL - RES + UNC` when every bin holds a
single forecast value.

- Domain: binary outcomes; grouping by distinct forecast values.
- Meaning: miscalibration minus discrimination plus base-rate uncertainty.
- Code: `validation.scoring.brier_decomposition(bins=None)`.
- Source: [REF-MURPHY-1973].
  - [REF-MURPHY-1973](../references.md#ref-murphy-1973): Allan H. Murphy. "A New Vector Partition of the Probability Score." *Journal of Applied Meteorology* 12, 595-600, 1973.
- Registry: [EQ-VAL-SCORE-BSDEC-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-val-score-bsdec-001--murphy-decomposition)

### EQ-VAL-SCORE-BSDEC-002 — Within-bin terms of the Brier decomposition

`BS = REL - RES + UNC + WBV - WBC` with
`WBV = (1/N) sum_k sum_(i in k) (f_i - fbar_k)^2` and
`WBC = (2/N) sum_k sum_(i in k) (f_i - fbar_k)(o_i - obar_k)`.

- Domain: any binning of the forecasts.
- Meaning: restores the exact identity when forecasts inside a bin differ;
  follows from expanding `f_i - o_i = (fbar_k - obar_k) + (f_i - fbar_k) -
  (o_i - obar_k)` inside each bin.
- Code: `validation.scoring.brier_decomposition`,
  `validation.BrierDecomposition.identity_residual`.
- Source: [REF-STEPHENSON-COELHO-JOLLIFFE-2008].
  - [REF-STEPHENSON-COELHO-JOLLIFFE-2008](../references.md#ref-stephenson-coelho-jolliffe-2008): David B. Stephenson, Caio A. S. Coelho, and Ian T. Jolliffe. "Two Extra Components in the Brier Score Decomposition." *Weather and Forecasting* 23(4), 752-757, 2008.
- Registry: [EQ-VAL-SCORE-BSDEC-002](../equation-registry.md#eq-val-score-bsdec-002--within-bin-terms-of-the-brier-decomposition)
