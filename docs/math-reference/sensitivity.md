### EQ-SENS-LOCAL-001 — Central finite-difference sensitivity

`partial_i f(x) ~= [f(x+h_i e_i)-f(x-h_i e_i)]/(2 h_i)`.

- Domain: positive finite step, finite model evaluations, and sufficient local
  smoothness for the usual second-order truncation interpretation.
- Meaning: measures local slope at one point; it does not average over the
  uncertain input distribution and charges two model calls per coordinate.
- Code: `sensitivity.central_difference_gradient`.
- Source: [REF-FORNBERG-1988].

### EQ-SENS-FIRST-001 — First-order Sobol index

`S_i=Var(E[Y|X_i])/Var(Y)`.

- Domain: [EQ-SENS-ANOVA-001] with positive output variance.
- Meaning: fraction of output variance explained by input `i` alone.
- Code: `sensitivity.estimate_sobol_indices` uses a Saltelli pick-freeze
  estimator `V_i = (1/N) sum (f_B - mean_f)(f_ABi - f_A)` with `f_B` centred
  on the pooled sample mean (the raw product's variance grows with the
  square of the output mean), and retains finite-sample values without
  clipping.

### EQ-SENS-DGSM-001 — Derivative-based global sensitivity measure

`nu_i = E[(d f / d x_i)^2]`, estimated by `mean_k (d f/d x_i)(x_k)^2` over
points `x_k` of the input law.

- Domain: `f` differentiable almost everywhere with square-integrable
  partial derivatives.
- Meaning: average squared local sensitivity of the output to input `i`.
- Code: `sensitivity.dgsm`.
- Source: [REF-SOBOL-KUCHERENKO-2009].

### EQ-SENS-DGSM-002 — Poincare upper bound on the total Sobol index

`S_Ti <= C(mu_i) nu_i / D`, `D = Var f(X)`, with `C = (b - a)^2 / pi^2`
for `Uniform(a, b)` (`nu_i / (pi^2 D)` on `[0, 1]`) and `C = sigma^2` for
`Normal(mu, sigma)`.

- Domain: independent inputs; the constants are the optimal Poincare
  constants of the two laws (equality for `cos(pi x)` on `[0, 1]` and for
  linear functions of normal inputs).
- Meaning: a small bound certifies an unimportant input; a large bound
  proves nothing.
- Code: `sensitivity.dgsm`, `sensitivity.poincare_constant`.
- Source: [REF-SOBOL-KUCHERENKO-2009] and [REF-LAMBONI-ETAL-2013].
  - [REF-SOBOL-KUCHERENKO-2009](../references.md#ref-sobol-kucherenko-2009): I. M. Sobol' and S. Kucherenko. "Derivative Based Global Sensitivity Measures and Their Link with Global Sensitivity Indices." *Mathematics and Computers in Simulation* 79(10), 3009-3017, 2009.

"Derivative-Based Global Sensitivity Measures: General Links with Sobol' Indices and Numerical Tests." *Mathematics and Computers in Simulation* 87, 45-54, 2013.

## `probstat.sensitivity.given_data`

### EQ-SENS-GIVEN-001 — Given-data first-order index by class partition

Sort by `x_i`, cut into `M` equal-count classes; `SSB = sum_m n_m
(ybar_m - ybar)^2`, `SST = sum (y_k - ybar)^2`, `SSW = SST - SSB`; raw
`eta^2 = SSB/SST`; corrected `epsilon^2 = 1 - (n-1)/(n-M) SSW/SST`.

- Domain: i.i.d. rows, `2 <= M <= n/2`; inputs may be dependent. Tied
  `x_i` values stay in one class (a cut inside a run of ties moves to its
  end), and `M` in the correction is the number `M_i` of classes used.
- Meaning: `Var(E[Y|X_i])/Var(Y)` from existing runs; the raw ratio is
  biased up by about `(M-1)(1-S_i)/(n-1)`, the class averaging biases it
  down by `O(1/M^2)` for a smooth conditional mean.
- Code: `sensitivity.given_data_first_order`.
- Source: [REF-PLISCHKE-BORGONOVO-SMITH-2013] and [REF-KELLEY-1935].
  - [REF-PLISCHKE-BORGONOVO-SMITH-2013](../references.md#ref-plischke-borgonovo-smith-2013): Elmar Plischke, Emanuele Borgonovo, and Curtis L. Smith. "Global Sensitivity Measures from Given Data." *European Journal of Operational Research* 226(3), 536-550, 2013.
  - [REF-KELLEY-1935](../references.md#ref-kelley-1935): Truman L. Kelley. "An Unbiased Correlation Ratio Measure." *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America* 21(9), 554-559, 1935.

## `probstat.sensitivity.model_based`

### EQ-SENS-BOOT-001 — Bootstrap percentile intervals for Sobol indices

Resample the design rows `k = 1..N` with replacement `B` times, recompute
[EQ-SENS-FIRST-001] and [EQ-SENS-TOTAL-001] on each resample from the stored
outputs `f(A)`, `f(B)`, `f(A_B^(i))`, and report the `(1-c)/2` and `(1+c)/2`
quantiles of the replicates.

- Domain: independent design rows; percentile intervals assume the bootstrap
  distribution approximates the sampling distribution, which is weakest for
  indices near zero and for strongly skewed outputs.
- Meaning: quantifies Monte Carlo error of the indices at no extra model cost.
- Code: `sensitivity.sobol_analysis`.

### EQ-SENS-MORRIS-001 — Morris elementary effects

`EE_i = [f(x + Delta e_i) - f(x)] / Delta`, `mu*_i = mean |EE_i|`,
`sigma_i = sd(EE_i)` over `r` one-at-a-time trajectories on a `p`-level grid
with `Delta = p / (2(p-1))` in the unit (probability) scale.

- Domain: independent inputs; effects are measured on the probability scale
  of each input.
- Meaning: cheap screening (`r(d+1)` runs); large `mu*` means influential,
  large `sigma` means nonlinear or interacting.
- Code: `sensitivity.morris_screening`.
- Source: [REF-MORRIS-1991] and [REF-CAMPOLONGO-CARIBONI-SALTELLI-2007].
  - [REF-MORRIS-1991](../references.md#ref-morris-1991): Max D. Morris. "Factorial Sampling Plans for Preliminary Computational Experiments." *Technometrics* 33(2), 161-174, 1991.

### EQ-SENS-RQMC-001 — Replicate (randomized QMC) intervals for Sobol indices

Split the `N` rows into `R` independent randomizations of `m = N // R` rows,
estimate the index on each (`S^(r)`), and report
`Sbar = mean_r S^(r)` with `Sbar +- t_(R-1, (1+c)/2) s_R / sqrt(R)`.

- Domain: `R >= 2`; any randomized design (scrambled Sobol, randomized
  Halton, LHS, Monte Carlo); replicates must use independent
  randomizations.
- Meaning: honest error bars for quasi-random designs, whose rows are not
  i.i.d. and must not be bootstrapped.
- Code: `sensitivity.sobol_analysis`, `sensitivity.sobol_analysis_all`.
- Source: [REF-L-ECUYER-2018] and [REF-OWEN-2013].
  - [REF-L-ECUYER-2018](../references.md#ref-l-ecuyer-2018): Pierre L'Ecuyer. "Randomized Quasi-Monte Carlo: An Introduction for Practitioners." In *Monte Carlo and Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods (MCQMC 2016)*, Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics 241, 29-52, 2018.

## `probstat.sensitivity.shapley`

### EQ-SENS-SHAPLEY-001 — Shapley effect

`Sh_i = (1/Var Y) sum_(J subset D\{i}) [|J|! (d-|J|-1)! / d!] (c(J u {i}) - c(J))`
`= (1/(d! Var Y)) sum_(pi) [c(P_i(pi) u {i}) - c(P_i(pi))]`, where `P_i(pi)` is
the set of inputs preceding `i` in permutation `pi`; `c(empty) = 0`,
`c(D) = Var Y`, so `sum_i Sh_i = 1`.

- Domain: square-integrable output; any joint input law.
- Meaning: the Shapley value of the variance game; with independent inputs
  and no interactions it equals the first-order and total Sobol indices.
- Code: `sensitivity.exact_shapley_values`, `sensitivity.shapley_effects`,
  `sensitivity.linear_gaussian_shapley`.
- Source: [REF-OWEN-2014] and [REF-SONG-NELSON-STAUM-2016].
  - [REF-OWEN-2014](../references.md#ref-owen-2014): Art B. Owen. "Sobol' Indices and Shapley Value." *SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification* 2(1), 245-251, 2014.
  - [REF-SONG-NELSON-STAUM-2016](../references.md#ref-song-nelson-staum-2016): Eunhye Song, Barry L. Nelson, and Jeremy Staum. "Shapley Effects for Global Sensitivity Analysis: Theory and Computation." *SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification* 4(1), 1060-1083, 2016.
- Registry: [EQ-SENS-SHAPLEY-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-sens-shapley-001--shapley-effect)

### EQ-SENS-SHAPLEY-002 — Conditional-variance cost and its estimator

`c(J) = E[Var(Y | X_(-J))]` gives the same Shapley values as
`Var(E[Y | X_J])`. Estimate:
`c_hat(J) = (1/N_O) sum_(o) s^2(Y(X_J^(o,1..N_I), X_(-J)^(o)))` with outer
draws `X_(-J)^(o)` from their marginal, inner draws
`X_J^(o,i) ~ X_J | X_(-J)^(o)`, and unbiased inner sample variance `s^2`.

- Domain: `N_I >= 2`; conditional sampling of `X_J` given `X_(-J)` must be
  available.
- Meaning: an unbiased estimator of the cost that needs only `N_I` inner
  draws; exact enumeration costs `N_V + (2^d - 2) N_O N_I` evaluations and
  random permutations `N_V + m (d - 1) N_O N_I`.
- Code: `sensitivity.shapley_effects`.
- Source: [REF-SONG-NELSON-STAUM-2016].
  - [REF-SONG-NELSON-STAUM-2016](../references.md#ref-song-nelson-staum-2016): Eunhye Song, Barry L. Nelson, and Jeremy Staum. "Shapley Effects for Global Sensitivity Analysis: Theory and Computation." *SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification* 4(1), 1060-1083, 2016.

### EQ-SENS-SHAPLEY-004 — Shapley effects of a linear Gaussian model

For `Y = b'X`, `X ~ N(mu, Sigma)`, `s = Sigma b`:
`Var(E[Y | X_J]) = s_J' Sigma_JJ^-1 s_J`, `Var Y = b' Sigma b`. For `d = 2`
with standard deviations `sigma_1, sigma_2` and correlation `rho`,
`Sh_1 = (b1^2 sigma_1^2 (1 - rho^2/2) + rho b1 b2 sigma_1 sigma_2 +
b2^2 sigma_2^2 rho^2/2) / Var Y`.

- Domain: positive-definite `Sigma`, `Var Y > 0`.
- Meaning: the correlated share `2 rho b1 b2 sigma_1 sigma_2` is split
  equally and each own share loses `rho^2/2` of itself to the partner;
  the two-dimensional formula was derived and checked numerically
  against the subset enumeration for this release.
- Code: `sensitivity.linear_gaussian_shapley`.
- Source: [REF-OWEN-PRIEUR-2017] and [REF-IOOSS-PRIEUR-2019].
  - [REF-OWEN-PRIEUR-2017](../references.md#ref-owen-prieur-2017): Art B. Owen and Clémentine Prieur. "On Shapley Value for Measuring Importance of Dependent Inputs." *SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification* 5(1), 986-1002, 2017.
  - [REF-IOOSS-PRIEUR-2019](../references.md#ref-iooss-prieur-2019): Bertrand Iooss and Clémentine Prieur. "Shapley Effects for Sensitivity Analysis with Correlated Inputs: Comparisons with Sobol' Indices, Numerical Estimation and Applications." *International Journal for Uncertainty Quantification* 9(5), 493-514, 2019.
- Registry: [EQ-SENS-SHAPLEY-004](../equation-registry.md#eq-sens-shapley-004--shapley-effects-of-a-linear-gaussian-model)

### EQ-SENS-SHAPLEY-005 — Standard error of an estimated Shapley effect

With exact enumeration `Sh_i = A_i / V + 1/d`, where
`A_i = sum_(J proper, non-empty) w_iJ c_hat(J)` collects the estimated
costs (signed subset weights `w_iJ`) and `V` is the estimated `Var Y`, which
also enters `c(D)`. The costs and `V` come from independent draws, so the
delta method gives
`Var(Sh_i) ~ sum_J w_iJ^2 Var(c_hat(J)) / V^2 + (A_i / V)^2 Var(V) / V^2`.
With `m` random permutations `Sh_i = B_i / V + L_i / m` (`L_i` permutations
that end in `i`, whose last increment is `V - c_hat(P_i)`); the permutation
spread `s_i^2` of the increments gives `Var(Sh_i | V) ~ s_i^2 / (m V^2)`,
and the same delta term `(B_i / V)^2 Var(V) / V^2` is added.
`Var(V) ~ (m_4 - V^2) / N_V` from the `N_V` variance draws.

- Domain: `N_V >= 2`; `N_O >= 2` for exact enumeration (otherwise the cost
  variances are unknown and the error is `nan`).
- Meaning: the normalizing variance is shared by every effect, so its
  sampling error does not average out over subsets or permutations; leaving
  it out understates the error of the permutation estimator and, for exact
  enumeration, misstates it in either direction because `V` cancels in part
  between `c(D)` and the denominator.
- Code: `sensitivity.shapley_effects`.
- Source: [REF-SONG-NELSON-STAUM-2016] (the estimators) and
  [REF-CASELLA-BERGER-2002, Section 5.5.4] (the delta method).
  - [REF-SONG-NELSON-STAUM-2016](../references.md#ref-song-nelson-staum-2016): Eunhye Song, Barry L. Nelson, and Jeremy Staum. "Shapley Effects for Global Sensitivity Analysis: Theory and Computation." *SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification* 4(1), 1060-1083, 2016.
  - [REF-CASELLA-BERGER-2002](../references.md#ref-casella-berger-2002): George Casella and Roger L. Berger. *Statistical Inference*. 2nd edition. Duxbury, 2002.
- Registry: [EQ-SENS-SHAPLEY-005](../equation-registry.md#eq-sens-shapley-005--standard-error-of-an-estimated-shapley-effect)

## `probstat.sensitivity.stochastic`

### EQ-SENS-STOCH-001 — Variance decomposition of a stochastic simulator

`Var(Y) = Var(m(X)) + E[Var(Y | X)]`, `m(x) = E[Y | X = x]`;
`S_i = V_i / Var(m)`, `ST_i = T_i / Var(m)` with `V_i = Var(E[m(X) | X_i])`,
`T_i = E[Var(m(X) | X_(~i))]`; output shares `V_i / Var(Y)` and the
intrinsic-noise index `S_omega = E[Var(Y | X)] / Var(Y)`.

- Domain: `Y = g(X, omega)` with independent inputs `X` and intrinsic
  randomness `omega` independent of `X`; finite `Var(Y)`, `Var(m) > 0`.
- Meaning: `V_i / Var(Y)` is the first-order index of `Y` when `omega` is
  treated as an extra input, and `S_omega` is the total effect of `omega`;
  indices of `m` describe the controllable part alone.
- Code: `sensitivity.stochastic_sobol_indices`,
  `sensitivity.StochasticSobolResult`.
- Source: [REF-IOOSS-RIBATET-MARREL-2009, Equations 15, 19, 20, and 21] and

### EQ-SENS-STOCH-002 — Replicate mean and within-point variance

At design point `x` with `R >= 2` independent runs `y_1..y_R`:
`ybar(x) = (1/R) sum_r y_r`, `s^2(x) = (1/(R-1)) sum_r (y_r - ybar)^2`, so
`E[s^2(x)] = Var(Y | X = x)` and `Var(ybar(x) | x) = Var(Y | X = x) / R`.

- Domain: runs at one point use independent seeds; runs at different
  points are independent.
- Meaning: the within-point variance estimates the intrinsic noise without
  bias, which the estimators of [EQ-SENS-STOCH-003] subtract.
- Code: `sensitivity.stochastic._replicate`.
- Source: [REF-IOOSS-RIBATET-MARREL-2009, Section 3.2] (dispersion
  component `Var(Y | X)`).
  - [REF-IOOSS-RIBATET-MARREL-2009](../references.md#ref-iooss-ribatet-marrel-2009): Bertrand Iooss, Mathieu Ribatet, and Amandine Marrel. "Global Sensitivity Analysis of Stochastic Computer Models with Joint Metamodels." Preprint arXiv:0802.0443v3, 2009. [https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.0443](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.0443).
- Registry: [EQ-SENS-STOCH-002](../equation-registry.md#eq-sens-stoch-002--replicate-mean-and-within-point-variance)

### EQ-SENS-STOCH-003 — Noise-corrected pick-freeze estimators

With design matrices `A`, `B`, `A_B^(i)` of `N` rows and `c` the mean of
`ybar` over `A` and `B`:
`Vhat_i = (1/N) sum_k (ybar_B,k - c)(ybar_ABi,k - ybar_A,k)`,
`That_i = (1/(2N)) sum_k [(ybar_A,k - ybar_ABi,k)^2 - (s^2_A,k + s^2_ABi,k)/R]`,
`noise = mean(s^2)` over all design points,
`Var(m)hat = var(ybar over A and B) - noise / R`.

- Domain: as [EQ-SENS-STOCH-001]; noise independent across design points.
- Meaning: independent noise cancels in the expectation of the first-order
  product, and the subtracted terms remove the noise inflation of the Jansen
  total-effect estimator and of the variance of replicate means.
- Code: `sensitivity.stochastic_sobol_indices`.
- Source: [REF-SALTELLI-2010] and [REF-JANSEN-1999] (pick-freeze
  estimators), applied to replicate means with the noise correction of
  [EQ-SENS-STOCH-002].
  - [REF-SALTELLI-2010](../references.md#ref-saltelli-2010): Andrea Saltelli, Paola Annoni, Ivano Azzini, Francesca Campolongo, Marco Ratto, and Stefano Tarantola. "Variance Based Sensitivity Analysis of Model Output: Design and Estimator for the Total Sensitivity Index." *Computer Physics Communications* 181(2), 259-270, 2010. DOI: [10.1016/j.cpc.2009.09.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2009.09.018).
  - [REF-JANSEN-1999](../references.md#ref-jansen-1999): Michiel J. W. Jansen. "Analysis of Variance Designs for Model Output." *Computer Physics Communications* 117(1-2), 35-43, 1999.
