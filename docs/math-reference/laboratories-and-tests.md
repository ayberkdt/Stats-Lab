### EQ-LAB-INPUT-002 — Skewness and kurtosis conventions of R's `e1071`

Type 1: `g1 = m3 / m2^(3/2)`, `g2 = m4 / m2^2 - 3`; type 3 (the default of
`e1071::skewness` and `e1071::kurtosis`): `b1 = m3 / s^3 = g1 ((n-1)/n)^(3/2)`
and `b2 = m4 / s^4 - 3 = (g2 + 3)(1 - 1/n)^2 - 3`, with
`m_k = n^-1 sum (x_i - xbar)^k` and `s` the standard deviation with divisor
`n - 1`.

- Domain: at least two observations with positive `m2`.
- Meaning: the same moment ratios with the population (`n`) or the sample
  (`n - 1`) standard deviation; the two conventions differ by a factor that
  tends to one as `n` grows. `statistics.skewness` and
  `statistics.excess_kurtosis` return type 1.
- Code: `examples.input_modeling_lab.describe`.
- Source: [REF-JOANES-GILL-1998], [REF-R-E1071].
  - [REF-JOANES-GILL-1998](../references.md#ref-joanes-gill-1998): D. N. Joanes and C. A. Gill. "Comparing Measures of Sample Skewness and Kurtosis." *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series D (The Statistician)* 47(1), 183-189, 1998.
  - [REF-R-E1071](../references.md#ref-r-e1071): David Meyer, Evgenia Dimitriadou, Kurt Hornik, Andreas Weingessel, and Friedrich Leisch. *e1071: Misc Functions of the Department of Statistics, Probability Theory Group (Formerly: E1071), TU Wien*. R package version 1.7-17, 2025. The `type` argument of `skewness` and `kurtosis` (types 1 to 3 after Joanes and Gill; type 3 is the default), read from the package help pages of version 1.7-17. DOI: [10.32614/CRAN.package.e1071](https://doi.org/10.32614/CRAN.package.e1071).
- Registry: [EQ-LAB-INPUT-002](../equation-registry.md#eq-lab-input-002--skewness-and-kurtosis-conventions-of-rs-e1071)

### EQ-LAB-INPUT-001 — Exact law of a block maximum

For `M = max(X_1, ..., X_m)` with independent `X_j ~ F`:
`P(M <= x) = F(x)^m`, density `m F(x)^(m-1) f(x)`, and quantile
`F^-1(p^(1/m))`.

- Domain: independent and identically distributed `X_j`; `m >= 1`;
  `0 < p < 1`.
- Meaning: the reference law against which fitted families, kernel
  estimates, and design values of simulated block maxima are judged; it
  is exact for every `m`, whereas the GEV law is its limit as `m` grows.
- Code: `examples.input_modeling_lab.exact_maximum_cdf`,
  `examples.input_modeling_lab.exact_maximum_pdf`,
  `examples.input_modeling_lab.exact_maximum_quantile`.
- Source: [REF-COLES-2001, Section 3.1].
  - [REF-COLES-2001](../references.md#ref-coles-2001): Stuart Coles. *An Introduction to Statistical Modeling of Extreme Values*. Springer, 2001.
- Registry: [EQ-LAB-INPUT-001](../equation-registry.md#eq-lab-input-001--exact-law-of-a-block-maximum)

### EQ-LAB-INPUT-003 — Integrated squared error of a density estimate

`ISE(h) = int (f_h(x) - f(x))^2 dx`.

- Domain: `f_h` and `f` square integrable; evaluated by adaptive
  Gauss-Kronrod quadrature on an interval that holds all but a negligible
  part of both densities.
- Meaning: the loss of one kernel estimate from one sample against the
  true density; least-squares (unbiased) cross-validation estimates
  `ISE(h) - int f^2` without knowing `f` [EQ-STAT-KDE-005].
- Code: `examples.input_modeling_lab.integrated_squared_error`.
- Source: [REF-BOWMAN-1984].

### EQ-LAB-PARA-001 — Drag-free landing abscissa

`y = x_0 + s_p sqrt(2 z_a / g)`.

- Domain: no drag and no wind, release with horizontal speed `s_p` and zero
  vertical speed from height `z_a > 0`, constant gravity `g`.
- Meaning: the trajectory is a parabola, so Heun and RK4 integrate it
  exactly and the cubic Hermite interpolant locates the landing to
  rounding; explicit Euler errs at order 1.
- Code: `examples.parachute_vvuq_lab.drag_free_landing`.
- Source: [REF-HALLIDAY-RESNICK-WALKER-2014, projectile motion].

### EQ-LAB-PARA-002 — Linear-drag landing through the Lambert W function

With `m x'' = m g - k (x' - s_w)` and `tau = m / k`:
`X(t) = x_0 + s_w t + (s_p - s_w) tau (1 - e^(-t/tau))`,
`Z(t) = z_a - g tau t + g tau^2 (1 - e^(-t/tau))`; `Z(T) = 0` at
`T = tau (1 + c + W_0(-e^(-(1 + c))))` with `c = z_a / (g tau^2)`, and the
landing abscissa is `X(T)`. `W_0` is computed by Halley's iteration from
the branch-point series `-1 + p - p^2/3 + 11 p^3/72`,
`p = sqrt(2 (e x + 1))`.

- Domain: `m, k, z_a > 0`, constant wind `s_w`; the principal branch gives
  the positive root (the branch `W_{-1}` gives a root at negative time).
- Meaning: an exact solution with drag, used for code verification of the
  integrator: the observed orders against it must be 1, 2, and 4.
- Code: `examples.parachute_vvuq_lab.linear_drag_landing`,
  `examples.parachute_vvuq_lab.lambert_w0`.
- Source: [REF-CORLESS-ETAL-1996].
  - [REF-CORLESS-ETAL-1996](../references.md#ref-corless-etal-1996): Robert M. Corless, Gaston H. Gonnet, David E. G. Hare, David J. Jeffrey, and Donald E. Knuth. "On the Lambert W Function." *Advances in Computational Mathematics* 5, 329-359, 1996. Branches, the branch-point series, and Halley's iteration. DOI: [10.1007/BF02124750](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02124750).
- Registry: [EQ-LAB-PARA-002](../equation-registry.md#eq-lab-para-002--linear-drag-landing-through-the-lambert-w-function)

### EQ-LAB-PARA-004 — Euler stability limit of the drag transient

`dt <= m / (A |v_r|)`, `|v_r| = |s_p - s_w|` at release.

- Domain: quadratic drag `-(A/m) |v_r| v_r`, whose Jacobian
  `-(A/m) (|v_r| I + v_r v_r^T / |v_r|)` has eigenvalues `-(A/m)|v_r|` and
  `-2 (A/m)|v_r|`; the relative speed is largest at release.
- Meaning: explicit Euler is stable for `|1 + dt lambda| <= 1`, so the
  stiffest eigenvalue `-2 (A/m)|v_r|` bounds the step.
- Code: `examples.parachute_vvuq_lab.stability_limit`.
- Source: [REF-HAIRER-WANNER-1996, Section IV.2].
  - [REF-HAIRER-WANNER-1996](../references.md#ref-hairer-wanner-1996): Ernst Hairer and Gerhard Wanner. *Solving Ordinary Differential Equations II: Stiff and Differential-Algebraic Problems*. Second revised edition. Springer Series in Computational Mathematics 14. Springer, Berlin, Heidelberg, 1996. ISBN 978-3-540-60452-5. Section IV.2 gives the stability analysis of explicit Runge-Kutta methods, including the stability region `|1 + h lambda| <= 1` of explicit Euler.

### EQ-LAB-PARA-005 — Verified error bound of a production step

`B(dt) = |y(dt) - y_ext| + U`, with the extrapolated value `y_ext` and the
expanded numerical uncertainty `U` of the finest grid from the solution
verification of the grids `dt, dt/2, dt/4` [EQ-VV-UNUM-001].

- Domain: three grids in the asymptotic range; outside it `B` can
  underestimate the error, which the laboratory checks against a reference.
- Meaning: an error bound for the coarse production step obtained from two
  extra runs, so that the step can be chosen against a tolerance.
- Code: `examples.parachute_vvuq_lab.step_bound`.
- Source: [REF-ROACHE-1998, Chapters 4-5]; [REF-ROY-OBERKAMPF-2011].

### EQ-PROC-MC-007 — Gambler's ruin (validation reference)

Walk on `{0..N}` with up-probability `p`, `q = 1 - p`, `r = q / p`:
`Pr(hit N | X_0 = i) = (1 - r^i) / (1 - r^N)` and
`E[T | X_0 = i] = i / (q - p) - (N / (q - p)) (1 - r^i) / (1 - r^N)` for
`p != 1/2`; `i / N` and `i (N - i)` for `p = 1/2`.

- Domain: `0 < p < 1`, absorbing barriers `0` and `N`.
- Meaning: closed-form absorption probabilities and expected durations that
  validate [EQ-PROC-MC-005].
- Code: `tests/test_stochastic_processes.py` (validation only).
- Source: [REF-ROSS-2019, Chapter 3] and [REF-NORRIS-1997, Section 1.3].
  - [REF-ROSS-2019](../references.md#ref-ross-2019): Sheldon M. Ross. *A First Course in Probability*. 10th edition. Pearson, 2019.
  - [REF-NORRIS-1997](../references.md#ref-norris-1997): J. R. Norris. *Markov Chains*. Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics. Cambridge University Press, 1997.
- Registry: [EQ-PROC-MC-007](../equation-registry.md#eq-proc-mc-007--gamblers-ruin-validation-reference)
