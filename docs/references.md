# Bibliography

Citation keys are stable project identifiers. A locator in a docstring or
equation record narrows the relevant part of a source.

## REF-CASELLA-BERGER-2002

George Casella and Roger L. Berger. *Statistical Inference*. 2nd edition.
Duxbury, 2002.

## REF-DEVROYE-1986

Luc Devroye. *Non-Uniform Random Variate Generation*. Springer, 1986.
DOI: [10.1007/978-1-4613-8643-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4613-8643-8).

## REF-KOLMOGOROV-1956

A. N. Kolmogorov. *Foundations of the Theory of Probability*. 2nd English
edition, translated by Nathan Morrison. Chelsea Publishing Company, 1956.

## REF-MATPLOTLIB

John D. Hunter. "Matplotlib: A 2D Graphics Environment." *Computing in Science
& Engineering* 9(3), 90-95, 2007.
DOI: [10.1109/MCSE.2007.55](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCSE.2007.55).

## REF-NIST-DLMF

NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, Chapter 7, "Error Functions,
Dawson's and Fresnel Integrals." National Institute of Standards and
Technology. [https://dlmf.nist.gov/7](https://dlmf.nist.gov/7).

## REF-NIST-EDA

NIST/SEMATECH. *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*, Section 1.3,
"Exploratory Data Analysis." National Institute of Standards and Technology.
[https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda3.htm](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda3.htm).

## REF-PYTHON-AST
